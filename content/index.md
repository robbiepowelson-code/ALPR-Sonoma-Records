A public archive of every document produced by the City of Santa Rosa in response to California Public Records Act requests about **Flock Safety** automated license plate readers (ALPR) and connected cameras. All 80 unique documents here were released through the city's [NextRequest portal](https://cityofsantarosaca.nextrequest.com/) across 12 separate requests filed between November 2025 and June 2026.

Use the **graph view** to explore how documents connect to requests and themes, or start with a theme below.

## What the records show

**The program.** Santa Rosa contracted with Flock Group Inc. in two phases. [[documents/f002713|Agreement F002713]] (approved January 9, 2024) purchased 31 Falcon ALPR cameras and 3 long-range Falcon LR units for **\$421,650 over three years**. [[documents/f002959|Agreement F002959]] (approved December 17, 2024) expanded the system with 70 more Falcon cameras, plus **14 Condor PTZ live-video cameras with LTE** — bringing the city's total commitment to roughly **\$1.32 million** and over 100 cameras. Both contracts set a 30-day data retention period and auto-renew unless cancelled 30 days before term end.

**The paper trail.** The [[themes/emails-vendor-communications|vendor emails]] document Flock's sales process, camera installations, API documentation, and integration with the city's Axon systems. The [[themes/audit-logs|audit logs]] show how often SRPD searches the system and what is shared across Flock's inter-agency network. The [[themes/camera-locations|encroachment permits]] place individual cameras at specific street corners.
