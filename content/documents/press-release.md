## Excerpt

> Santa Rosa Police @ nee © February 12-@ During Tuesday's City Council meeting discussion about immigration, Chief Cregan addressed several important issues, including the use of Automated License Plate Reader cameras, commonly called ALPR or Flock cameras. We want to take a moment to provide more clarity about how ALPR technology is used here in Santa Rosa. These tools help us solve crimes, recover stolen vehicles, and keep our community safer, but just as important is how responsibly we use them. Here are a few things we want you to know: -ALPR data is never used for immigration enforcement.
